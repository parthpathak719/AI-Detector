How to interpret this result

    DOCUMENT DETAIL

    Segment-by-segment analysis

    Scores describe model signals in each normalized segment. They do not establish authorship.

    UNDER THE HOOD

    One workflow. Three layers of insight.

    01 / DETECTION

    Read the patterns

    MuRIL captures multilingual context. Character n-grams capture local writing patterns. Together, they estimate whether text resembles human or AI writing.

    02 / ATTRIBUTION

    Compare known families

    AI-leaning content is compared with Gemini, Muse and Sarvam. The closest match is a model estimate, not a verified generator identity.

    03 / DOCUMENTS

    Go beyond one paragraph

    Long text is split into model-sized segments. The report combines their scores and lets you inspect each part. Document-level accuracy has not yet been benchmarked.
