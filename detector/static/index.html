    THE METHOD

    Language-aware.
    Evidence-led.

    A research tool for exploring patterns,
    not a verdict on who wrote something.

    01

    Meaning meets character patterns

    Frozen MuRIL embeddings capture context across languages. Character n-grams capture local spelling patterns. The 16 handcrafted features are excluded.

    02

    Two questions, separate predictions

    First: human or AI? Then, for AI predictions: which of Gemini, Muse or Sarvam is the closest match? Other generators may resemble these families.

    03

    Small datasets. Clear boundaries.

    Tuning keeps related source texts together. Scores are calibrated on validation data, but can still be wrong on unfamiliar domains, languages or edited text. Don’t use this as the sole basis for an academic or professional decision.
