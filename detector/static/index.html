    About this project

    MuRIL embeddings and character n-grams are used for human/AI detection. AI-leaning text is compared with three trained families: Gemini, Muse and Sarvam. Seven language settings are supported.

    Long documents are analyzed in segments. Technical documents, edited text and unseen generators can be misclassified, even with high scores.
